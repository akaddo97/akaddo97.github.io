What gets taught

A six-week curriculum. Fifteen topics, in four tracks, applied to a real business or personal tech problem.

The six weeks · 1:1 with AK

  1. Week 1 Kickoff and the tooling. The theory of what a large language model is actually doing.
  2. Week 2 Designing your own research prompts, and how AI search and retrieval works under the hood.
  3. Week 3 Prompt depth and iteration, plus enough data science to read what comes back critically.
  4. Week 4 Metaprompting, building agents and tool use, workflow compression, and context management.
  5. Week 5 Knowledge graphs (conceptually), and why capturing your own data compounds.
  6. Week 6 Knowledge graphs built for real, with RAG and retrieval wired in, closing the loop on your own work.

Group discussions

Two or three across the six weeks, alongside your 1:1s. Show-and-tell style, everyone has a chance to yap.
