For teams

Fluency work starts from the job you already do rather than from a curriculum. We go through your actual workflow, find where the time goes, and rebuild those steps around the AI that exists this week. You keep working tools at the end, not notes.

The upskilling cohort

I also run a small AI upskilling cohort, teaching young professionals practical AI skills. It is selective and free for the people I take on. Members commit to an hour a week, do the homework, and report back.

It runs across three lanes:

  • Health AI, for clinical and health-operations settings.
  • Financial-services AI, for finance, markets, and operators.
  • Marketing AI, for growth, content, and audience work.

Places are limited. If you want one, say which lane fits and what you want to build.
