Homepage options

Four ways to carry the three offerings

Each option is a complete homepage carrying your revised copy. They differ in how baking is made less prominent than the other two, not in colour. Every option ships an Edit button, so you can rewrite any wording in place and download a copy.

None of these is live. The homepage at akaddo97.github.io is unchanged apart from the new copy.

Option A

Two panes plus a quiet strip

Bespoke and Fluency as equal cards, baking a slim muted strip beneath. Closest to the old two-pane site. Hierarchy by area.

Option B

Three cards, one lighter

All three in a row of equal width, baking dashed and grey. Reads as a menu where one item is quieter. Hierarchy by weight.

Option C

Editorial rows

Full-width rows divided by hairlines, baking last and smaller. The most typographic, and the closest to the page as it stands. Hierarchy by order.

Option D

Split hero, baking in the footer

A near-black and cream two-colour split for Bespoke and Fluency only, with baking dropped out of the offering set into the footer. The boldest departure.
