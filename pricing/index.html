The method

  1. Discovery

    Map you and/or your team's tech stack. Define the problems you need to solve with this tech product.

  2. Choose the goals

    Pick a small number of demonstrable business goals to tailor the products that are built.

  3. Non-AI tech work

    If necessary (e.g. a structured database to allow for a language model to work well on your company data).

  4. Build a solution

    A tech product with an AI layer. Built end to end: from the data to the interface.

  5. Measure

    Against the goals from stage 2.
