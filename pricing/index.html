The method

Every engagement runs through the same five stages. The order matters: the measures are agreed before any building starts, so the plumbing work is a scoped dependency rather than a surprise discovered after signing.

  1. Diagnose

    Walk the systems that actually exist. Email, records, scheduling, whatever the finance runs on, and what talks to what. You get a map of your stack, which most owners have never seen written down.

  2. Choose the goals

    Pick a small number of demonstrable business goals before anything is built. Hours returned, response times, error rates, whatever you already track. Agreed in advance, in writing, chosen by you.

  3. Fix the plumbing

    The non-AI tech work. Integration, data hygiene, and the systems that do not currently talk to each other.

  4. Build the thing

    The AI layer, on top of a stack that can now support it. Built end to end, from the data layer to the interface.

  5. Measure

    Against the goals from stage 2, not against a new set chosen afterwards. You get a result you can point at.
